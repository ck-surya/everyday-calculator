A couple of good questions.

Can I use this for any currency?

The percentage calculation works for any currency. This version displays Indian rupees and rounds amounts to two decimal places.

Does this include taxes or delivery fees?

No. The result applies one discount to the price you enter. Add any applicable taxes or delivery charges separately.

What about two discounts in a row?

Calculate the first discount, then enter that final price as the original price for the second. Two discounts of 20% and 10% give a combined discount of 28%, rather than 30%.
